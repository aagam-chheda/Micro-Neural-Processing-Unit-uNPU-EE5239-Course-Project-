## 1. What this stage does

The 4x4 INT8 weight-stationary systolic matrix-multiply accelerator `unpu_top` as synthesisable SystemVerilog (PE, grid, input skew, output de-skew, DMA, activation and weight buffers, CSR, sequencer, APB slave, top). Timing contract of CLAUDE.md: A[m][k] arrives at PE(k,j) at cycle m+k+j, C[m][j] leaves column j at m+j+4, a whole row is valid at m+7, a pass of M rows takes M+7 cycles.
